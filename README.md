# ✦ MINI-GAME HUB

> **Play. Earn. Persist.**
> A scalable, terminal-based ecosystem engineered for continuous progression.

---

### 💎 The Coin Economy

Performance equals capital. Our centralized economic engine instantly calculates metrics and converts them into a persistent digital currency. Earn coins, build your balance, and drive your progression across the entire hub.

### 💾 Seamless Persistence

Your progress, permanently secured. Deep database integration ensures that every session state, high score, and coin transaction is safely stored, instantly retrievable, and ready for your next login.

### ⚙️ Enterprise Architecture

Built on strict, decoupled Object-Oriented principles. This isn't just a script—it's a robust, modular framework designed to scale effortlessly as new environments are added.
